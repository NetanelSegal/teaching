# ORT Smart-Home Manual

## Troubleshooting
If your smart-bulb is flashing red, it means there is a connectivity error with your Wi-Fi. Please reset your router and try again.

## Warranty
All products are covered by a 24-month hardware warranty. For claims, contact support@ort.com.

## Return Policy
You can return any product within 30 days of purchase if the seal is unbroken.
