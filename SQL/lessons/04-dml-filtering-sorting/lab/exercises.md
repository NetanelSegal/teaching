# Lab 04: The Talent Scout

## Goal
Practice filtering and sorting data to find specific records.

## Preparation
Imagine a table named `applicants` with:
- `id`
- `full_name`
- `experience_years`
- `expected_salary`
- `position` (e.g., 'Frontend', 'Backend', 'DevOps')

## Tasks

### 1. The Expert Search
Find all 'Backend' developers with more than 5 years of experience.

### 2. The Budget Filter
Find all 'Frontend' developers who expect a salary between 10,000 and 15,000.

### 3. The Shortlist
Find all 'DevOps' or 'Backend' developers. Sort them by experience (most experienced first) and show only the top 3.

---

## Instructions
Write your SQL queries and verify them against the `solution.sql`.
