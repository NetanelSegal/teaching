# Project 01: E-commerce Backend Design

## 🌟 The Challenge
You have been hired by a new startup, **"QuickShop,"** to build their database from scratch. They need to track customers, their products, and every order placed. Your job is to design a schema that ensures data integrity and allows for fast reporting.

## 🧠 Program Logic Flow
1.  **Define Entities**: Identify the core tables (Customers, Products, Orders).
2.  **Establish Relationships**: Use Primary and Foreign keys to link Orders to Customers and Products.
3.  **Apply Constraints**: Ensure prices are positive, emails are unique, and stock doesn't go below zero.
4.  **Seed Data**: Insert realistic sample data for testing.
5.  **Run Reports**: Write queries to find the "Top Customer," "Best Selling Product," and "Total Monthly Revenue."

## 🛠️ Implementation Tasks

### Phase 1: Schema Design (The Blueprint)
- **Task 1.1: Create Tables**
  - `customers`: `id`, `first_name`, `last_name`, `email` (Unique), `join_date`.
  - `products`: `id`, `name`, `description`, `price` (Check > 0), `stock`.
  - `orders`: `id`, `customer_id` (FK), `order_date`, `total_amount`.
  - `order_items`: `id`, `order_id` (FK), `product_id` (FK), `quantity`, `price_at_time`.

### Phase 2: Data & Integrity
- **Task 2.1: Populate the Store**
  - Insert at least 5 customers.
  - Insert at least 10 products across 3 categories.
- **Task 2.2: Simulate Orders**
  - Create 5 orders. Ensure one customer has multiple orders.
  - Ensure one order contains multiple items.

### Phase 3: Business Intelligence (The "Ask")
- **Task 3.1: The Revenue Report**
  - Calculate the total revenue generated by the store.
- **Task 3.2: Customer Loyalty**
  - Find the customer who has spent the most money.
- **Task 3.3: Inventory Alert**
  - List all products that have less than 5 items in stock.

## 🚀 Going Beyond (Stretch Goals)
- **Category Sales**: Add a `category` column to products and find the most profitable category.
- **Monthly Growth**: Write a query that shows total sales grouped by month.
- **Customer Tiers**: Use a `CASE` statement to label customers as 'Gold' (spent > $500) or 'Silver' (spent < $500).
